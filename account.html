My Athenaeum Account

Our library uses GitHub for hosted books and Cloudflare for the site and upcoming member accounts.

Cloudflare email sign-in is not active yet. The site owner must enable Cloudflare Access and allow specific member email addresses. After that, Access can email a one-time sign-in PIN; no phone SMS service or password is required.

Personal online shelves, reading progress, recommendations and shared notes will use the Cloudflare D1 database after Access sign-in and server-side identity verification have been connected. Until then, the Personal Hub works locally in the browser.
